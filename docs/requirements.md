# Requirements

## Public Website

### REQ-001 — School Identity

Present the school name, short description, identity, and primary call to action.

### REQ-002 — Profile

Explain the school's background, vision, mission, and relevant highlights.

### REQ-003 — Programs

Present the main educational programs or areas of study.

### REQ-004 — Facilities

Present important facilities and supporting infrastructure.

### REQ-005 — News

Provide a section for current school news and announcements.

### REQ-006 — Gallery

Provide a visual section for school activities and events.

### REQ-007 — Contact

Provide address, contact channels, and other useful visitor information.

### REQ-008 — Responsive Experience

The website must remain usable and readable on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

### REQ-009 — Basic SEO

Provide meaningful page metadata, semantic structure, and accessible content.

## Quality Requirements

- Keep the initial implementation lightweight.
- Avoid unnecessary dependencies.
- Use reusable components only where repetition or clarity justifies them.
- Content should be easy to replace with real school data.
