# Vision

## Why

Schools need a clear digital presence that makes important information easy to discover and gives prospective students and parents a trustworthy first impression.

## Problem

School information is often spread across social media, messaging apps, and static documents. Visitors need a single, structured place to understand the school.

## Solution

Build a focused school profile website that presents essential information in a clear, modern, responsive experience.

## Who

- Prospective students
- Parents and guardians
- Current students
- Alumni
- Teachers and school staff
- General visitors

## Value

- Clear school identity
- Easy access to important information
- Professional public presence
- Mobile-friendly communication

## Non-goals

The initial website is not an all-in-one school management system. Administrative workflows, academic management, payments, and other operational systems are outside the initial public website scope.
